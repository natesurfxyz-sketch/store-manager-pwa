Privacy Policy

Store Manager · Last updated: 21 November 2026

Store Manager helps shop owners manage products, stock, customers, orders and invoices. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Who we are

Store Manager is operated by its developer. You can contact us at voidseries.divine@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
  • Store information you enter: store name and logo, products, prices, stock levels, product photos, customer details (names, phone numbers, addresses, notes and social links), orders and invoices.
  • Device storage: the app keeps a copy of your store data on your device so it can work with a weak connection.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

We use your information only to provide the app: to sign you in, show your store data, sync it between your devices and staff, and let you create reports, invoices and receipts. We do not show ads and we do not sell personal information.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access only to scan barcodes or QR codes and to take product photos. Scanning happens on your device. Photos you choose are resized and saved to your store as product photos.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database and authentication service, and is separated by store so that only people who belong to your store can see it. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Sharing

Your data is shared with the other staff members you invite to your store, and with our hosting provider (Supabase) only to run the service. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Your customers' information

Store owners decide what customer information to enter. If you enter information about your customers, you are responsible for using it lawfully and for keeping it accurate.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account inside the app: open More → Settings → Delete account. You can also email us at the address above to ask for your account, or your whole store and its data, to be deleted. We will act on requests within a reasonable time.

Children

Store Manager is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
